The working loop

Brief. Build. Prove.

Describe what you want to make. The skill helps your agent shape the story, build it in your project, and check the result across screen sizes and motion preferences.

01

Find the story

Start with your subject, copy and images. Build the page around what the visitor should understand.

02

Direct the motion

Use motion to reveal a detail, explain a mechanism or connect one part of the story to the next.

03

Ship evidence

Inspect the actual page on desktop and mobile. Keep its story and actions available when motion is turned off.
