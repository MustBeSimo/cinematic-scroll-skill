OPTICAL STUDY

One landscape, a changing frame. Open the aperture to reveal the outer contours.

01 / Observe

Start with
a detail.

The narrow opening isolates a few contours. Before you see the whole landscape, follow the lines that pass through its centre.

A small frame concentrates your attention.

02 / Focus

Let the
world in.

Open the aperture and watch the six blades withdraw. More of the same terrain appears: a detail becomes part of a larger pattern.

Try the aperture at both ends of its range.

03 / Reframe

Same place.
New perspective.

As you move down the page, the contours turn within their frame. Return to the beginning to see the same landscape from your first viewpoint.

Reverse your scroll to retrace the view.
