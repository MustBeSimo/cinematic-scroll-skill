ATELIER·MARNE
Scroll = the walk
Permanent collection

A gallery you walk by scrolling.

No video. No baked frames. The room is rendered live — and the camera's pace is exactly your scroll's pace.

Hall I — Light

Walls that hold the warm.

Brass strips wash the plaster; the camera dollies the length of the hall as fast or slow as you choose.

Hall II — Form

Objects on plinths of shadow.

Each piece turns under its own spotlight as you pass — parallax in real depth, not layered images.

Through the arch.

A threshold opens. Slow down here.

Hall III — Scale

The room opens wide.

Ceiling lifts, light pools on the floor — the same engine, a bigger volume.

End of the walk

Every brief, its own gallery.
