01 — Acquire

Everything is noiseuntil you measure it.

The raw capture is honest and useless. Sensor grain, mains hum, thermal drift: the trace behind this page carries all of it, at a noise floor of −12 dB.

We do not clean an image. We reject what does not repeat. Each stage below removes one class of noise and records the tolerance we can now defend.

02 — Isolate

One light.Lock on it.

The corridor has exactly one source. The reticle finds it, and every other reading in the frame is now referenced to that 4000 K tube.

Mains hum at 50 Hz and its harmonics leave first. The noise floor drops past −48 dB and the trace behind you stops shivering. This is the moment the page is built around.
