# Direct a scroll story

Use this for new pages and substantial redesigns. For a small fix, preserve the
existing story and repair the affected beat.

## First preview without a questionnaire

Extract subject, audience, action, brand, and delivery format. State a concrete,
reversible assumption if needed: “I'll use your blue/ivory palette for a compact
product story with one exploded-view reveal and an enquiry action.” Proceed.
Ask only when the answer changes the product, available content, or scope.

The visual systems in [film-archetypes.md](film-archetypes.md) are reference points.
A local duotone/serif-sans/dense/underline contract, for example, survives when
adapting an airy monochrome example. Do not force a brand into a named theme.

## Beat sheet

Keep one row per meaningful change, not per animation:

| Beat | Visitor learns | Focal asset | Start → change → hold → exit | Mobile / static | Proof |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Orientation | What this is and why it matters | Product and title | Readable opening → restrained reveal → hold → flow | Same content, no pin | Top |
| Signature | A specific advantage | Product mechanism | Establish → expose mechanism → labels settle → release | Stacked before/after, captions | Mid-reveal and hold |
| Evidence | Why the claim is credible | Real detail or demonstration | Stable reading, optional local reveal | Stable reading | Midpoint |
| Action | What to do next | Working link or existing form | Quiet landing, no forced delay | Same action | Bottom |

This is a thinking aid, not a mandatory four-section layout. Never invent
statistics, testimonials, customer logos, or destinations to fill the table.

## Compose the intervals

A starting allocation for a scrubbed reveal: 0–20% establish, 20–55% transform,
55–80% readable hold, 80–100% release. Adjust to content. Test the 35% frame:
two half-visible scenes are not automatically a good transition. Keep text stable
while it must be read; supporting visuals can carry motion.

Pin only when holding a frame makes the explanation clearer. Estimate distance
from visible changes and reading load, then test with wheel and touch. Long scroll
costs the visitor time. Use free flow for reading and navigation; headings and
anchors must remain meaningful without pins.

## Assets and identity

Name a subject, material/light language, and one or two recurring motifs. Make the
signature specific: a lens assembly opens, a route connects real locations, a book
reveals its spreads. A generic chrome orb is not a substitute for product thinking.

Record each asset's source, crop/focal point, purpose, dimensions, loading priority,
and fallback. Supplied assets come first. If generation is available and authorized,
specify consistent light/materials and negative space for live text. Keep navigation
and factual claims in HTML. Do not require a paid image service for a first preview.

Complete a vertical slice before expanding: readable opening, signature change,
release, and mobile/static equivalent. Inspect these states to catch weak concepts
and geometry problems without imposing a design approval ceremony.
