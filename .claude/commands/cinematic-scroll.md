# /cinematic-scroll

Build cinematic, scroll-driven websites with the **`cinematic-scroll`** skill —
pinned chapter reveals, hero/multi-depth parallax, scroll-linked 3D tilt and
camera moves, and environment-morphing backgrounds. From a single self-contained
`.html` scroll section (Mode A) to a full Next.js release site with optional
AI-generated visuals (Mode B). The motion grammar is the constant; the aesthetic
is always the user's.

## When this fires

Use it when the request is for a scroll-driven or "cinematic" site, pinned/sticky
chapter sections, hero or multi-depth parallax, scroll-linked 3D tilt or camera
moves, an environment/background-morphing layout, a product story page, an
editorial commerce microsite, or a release/launch/drop page.

## Trigger

> Use cinematic-scroll to build a self-contained HTML pinned hero chapter for [YOUR BRAND].
