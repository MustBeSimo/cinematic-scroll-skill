Meridian

Meridian

One line around the earth.

A single incised line circles the vessel at the height of a resting hand. Everything else is left to the clay.

Form

Thrown in three pulls, trimmed at leather-hard, 240 mm tall, 1.9 kg.

Glaze

Ash and feldspar, reduction fired to cone 10. No two lines land in the same place.

Studio

Made in a converted signal box beside the railway. Twelve vessels a month.
