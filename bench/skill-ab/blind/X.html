Ferrymead Bakehouse

Bread on the tide.

We bake slow sourdough in the old ferry hall, with flour from two farms upriver and a starter that is older than the building's new roof.

The loaves

  • Estuary Loaf (country sourdough, 900 g)

  • Rye Tide (60% rye, caraway, 800 g)

  • Saltmarsh (sea-salt crust, wholemeal, 900 g)

  • Ferry Crumpets (six per pack, weekends only)

The subscription

  1. Choose one or two loaves a week.
  2. We bake Thursday night, you collect Friday from the hall or one of three pickup points along the river.
  3. Pause or cancel any week before Wednesday noon.
