# Show HN

Hacker News is the highest-leverage *and* highest-risk channel. The crowd is technical,
allergic to marketing, and rewards honesty + a real artifact they can click. Get it right
and it drives thousands of devs + a star spike in a day.

> **Show HN: Cinematic Scroll – give any coding agent the taste to build scroll sites**

- `Show HN: A free Agent Skill that makes Claude/Cursor art-direct cinematic scroll sites`

## Why this framing works on HN
- **First person, specific problem, no hype words.** ("revolutionary/stunning" = downvotes.)
- **Pre-empts the cynic** ("art-direction system, not magic button"; "wants a human eye").
- **Invites critique of the substance**, which drives comments → ranking.
- **Real clickable artifact** (5 live demos) above the fold.

## Timing & mechanics
- Post **Tue–Thu, ~8–10am US Eastern** (peak HN). Avoid weekends and Fridays.
- Submit, then post the comment within 60 seconds.
- **Do NOT ask for upvotes anywhere** (fastest way to get flagged/penalized).
- Sit on the thread for the first 3–4 hours and answer *every* comment, fast, technical, non-defensive. Engagement velocity = ranking.
- If it flops the first time, you may resubmit once after a few weeks (HN allows a second shot at Show HNs that got no attention). Don't spam it.
