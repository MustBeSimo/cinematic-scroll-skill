## Non-list directories worth a submission
- **GitHub topics** — already good (`agent-skills`, `claude-skill`, `gsap`…). Add `awwwards`, `scrollytelling`, `webgl`-adjacent? Only accurate ones. Consider `scrollytelling` + `web-design`.
- **GitHub repo "Releases"** — write a proper v2.1.0 release note with the video embedded (releases rank in Google).
- **dev.to / Hashnode / Medium** — cross-post the "how it works" article (see `../launch/README.md` → x10 roadmap). Canonical-link back to your site.
- **Bestofjs.org** — submit; it tracks trending JS projects and feeds newsletters.
- **Newsletter tips:** *JavaScript Weekly*, *Frontend Focus*, *Bytes.dev*, *TLDR Web Dev*, *Smashing*. Email a 2-line tip + the video link. These drive huge, qualified spikes and they actively want free OSS with a wow demo.
